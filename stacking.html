Include Mercenaries in Optimization

Leave this off when your mercenary quantities are small and you mainly want to optimize full Leadership and Dominance. Selected mercenaries are ignored by the optimizer and can be added manually in game.

Turn it on when your mercenary army is large enough to materially affect the battle. Mercenary squads can change death order and attack opportunities, which can also change the optimized Troop and Monster quantities.
