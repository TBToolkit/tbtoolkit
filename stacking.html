Battle Calculator

Choose a battle type and calculation method. Enter your army limits and combat bonuses, then select the units you want to use. The Battle Calculator can calculate a standard stack, follow a custom death order, or optimize supported Epic battles. PvP calculations include PvP health, strength, matchup bonuses, and revival costs when they apply.

Account level: The Battle Calculator is designed around fully researched Tier 9 accounts. Developing accounts can also use it effectively.

Research and talents: Calculations assume that relevant Health and Strength talent points are complete or distributed evenly, and that Army Modernization and Monster Boost research are complete.
