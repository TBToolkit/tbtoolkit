About TB Toolkit

A free player-created project built to share useful tools and information with the Total Battle community.

About the site

Why TB Toolkit exists

TB Toolkit collects practical calculators, reference data, and planning resources in one place. The site is designed to be simple to use on desktop and mobile devices.

The tools will remain free to use. There are no paywalls planned.

About Biff

Created by a player

Biff is a Total Battle player who enjoys data, strategy, and building tools that make repetitive game tasks easier. TB Toolkit is a way to share that work with other players.

Use the tools, share them, and build your own tools from the public data when it becomes available.
